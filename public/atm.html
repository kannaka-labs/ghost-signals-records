How this works, plainly

  • The buying is done by Coinbase, a licensed on-ramp. They check your identity, take your payment, and send the USDC straight to the address you typed. We mint a five-minute, single-use session for your address and nothing more.
  • Coinbase charges its own fee on the purchase (a spread plus a card or ACH fee). The ATM adds nothing on that leg.
  • The ATM's surcharge, like a real one, is on the swap desk: a small cut of each swap, shown before you confirm.
  • We never hold your money or your coins, and we cannot reverse a purchase. If something looks wrong, stop before you pay.
