    The USDC ATM

    Records here cost five dollars in USDC on Base, and you do not need ETH to pay: the store pays the gas. If you have no USDC yet, this machine has two legs.

    • Card in, USDC out. Coinbase takes the card and the identity check; the USDC lands in your own wallet. The machine takes nothing on this leg.
    • Swap to USDC. ETH, WETH, cbBTC or DAI on Base, swapped in one transaction from your wallet. The machine keeps one percent, shown on the quote before you sign.

    Nothing here holds your money. Both legs end with USDC in the wallet you name.
